## Limits

- Britannica pages could not be opened; several cited resources (a documents collection, a research volume) have no URL and could not be checked.
- The 1954 dismissal date differs by a day inside Wikipedia (29 or 30 May), so the question says 'late May'.
- The Bhashani and Bhutto distractors in `pak-007` and the 1952 names in `pak-010` were judged wrong from general history, not from an opened page.
