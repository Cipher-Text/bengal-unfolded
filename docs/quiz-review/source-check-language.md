## Fixes applied (2026-11-27)

1. **lan-023:** rewritten as "Who supervised construction of the permanent Shaheed Minar from 1957, assisted by Novera Ahmed?" (Hamidur Rahman; distractors Abul Kalam Shamsuddin and Azam Khan, both named on the Banglapedia Shaheed Minar page). Difficulty changed from hard to medium. The contested "designed" framing is gone.
2. **lan-004:** the question now says "ahead of the 21 February 1952 demonstrations" instead of giving 20 February.
3. **lan-001 and lan-010:** explanations now say "February 1948" instead of 25 February.
4. **lan-005:** correct option and explanation now read "the Dhaka University campus, close to the Medical College".

The verdicts in the table above describe the drafts before these fixes; the Bengali of the changed text is new and unreviewed. The review CSV was regenerated after the fixes.

## Limits

- The UNESCO page returned a bot check, so the claim that the day was first observed in 2000 is unchecked. No remaining question relies on it (`lan-024` was dropped), but the timeline entry does.
- Banglapedia gives Jinnah's arrival as 19 March and Wikipedia has him speaking on 24 March; the event timeline says 21 to 24 March. No question states a date for the visit.
- Several cited resources (books, research articles) were not opened; the checks above rest on the pages named in each row.
- The Bengali is unreviewed. Terms to check: প্রথম শহীদ মিনার, শহীদ দিবস, যুক্তফ্রন্ট, ২১ দফা.
