Date: 2026-11-24. Scope: draft questions `col-001` to `col-014` (topic `colonial-conquest-revenue-and-resistance`). The batch was drafted by Codex, rewritten by the lead, then two agents checked every question against open sources (Banglapedia and Wikipedia were opened; Britannica returned access errors, so no Britannica wording is verified). All 14 are READY for Bengali review; none is published (every question is `status: draft`).

Same method and limits as `source-check.md`: verdicts judge whether the correct answer is documented, whether each wrong option is clearly wrong, and whether each sentence of the explanation holds. Contested or varying details (dates, army sizes, death toll) were removed or stated as varying.

## Limits

- Several Wikipedia pages were read only in part. Britannica pages returned 403, so the Britannica resources cited in `resourceIds` could not be checked.
- The famine question rests on documented factors, but Banglapedia frames the revenue point as an argument about Company policy, and historians disagree about the death toll; the question avoids any death figure.
- The 1704 shift of the capital and the diwani grant date vary by source and are worded accordingly.
- The Bengali is unreviewed. New Bengali terms to check: জগৎ শেঠ, মকসুদাবাদ, শুজাউদ্দৌলা, দেওয়ান, নাজম-উদ-দৌলা.
