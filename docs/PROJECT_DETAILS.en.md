# Bengal Unfolded

## Project Goal

Bengal Unfolded exists to present the history of Bengal and Bangladesh as a connected human journey rather than a collection of disconnected events. The project focuses on memory, identity, language, resistance, and transformation.

## Why It Matters

History is often reduced to summaries, dates, and isolated lessons. This project aims to create a space where historical experience feels alive, meaningful, and emotionally legible for modern readers.

## Historical Focus

The project looks at the long arc of Bengal's story through defining moments such as partition, language, liberation, democratic struggle, and later civic movements. These moments are treated not as separate incidents, but as part of an evolving national and cultural consciousness.

## Values

- clarity over noise
- historical sensitivity
- accessibility across languages
- respect for memory and lived experience
- connection between past and present

## Mission

The mission of Bengal Unfolded is to make history easier to understand, easier to feel, and easier to revisit through a thoughtful bilingual presentation of Bengal's historical journey.

## Vision

The vision is to become a meaningful historical storytelling platform where readers can explore Bengal's past with depth, dignity, and context.
