## Publishing guardrails

- Do not publish automatically from scores.
- Do not create a second page for an existing event, figure, place, or topic intent.
- Do not add uncited claims or convert disputed history into settled fact.
- Every factual change must be mirrored in EN and BN when both locales exist.
- Track the canonical URL, target query, source set, title/description change, publish date, and recheck date in `docs/SEO_GSC_OPTIMIZATION_LOG.md`.
